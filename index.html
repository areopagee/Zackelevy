A consulting company rooted in the science of living systems

Zackelevy LLC provides biology and agriculture consulting. Our work connects what is known about soil, plants, and ecosystems with the practical decisions people make on the land.

We are located at 312 W 2nd St in Casper, Wyoming, and we work with clients through conversation first: understanding the question, the setting, and the limits of what is known before recommending a direction.

What we do and do not claim

This website describes the general scope of our consulting. It does not list certifications, awards, partnerships, or measured results. Where a project calls for specialized testing or a licensed professional, we will say so and help you find the right one.

Our approach

We keep the process simple and honest. Each project moves through the same four stages.

  1. Listen

    We learn about your land, your goals, and the decision in front of you.

  2. Review

    We look at available information and relevant research, and note what is uncertain.

  3. Advise

    We explain options in plain language, with the reasoning and the trade-offs.

  4. Follow up

    We agree on next steps and refer you to specialists when the question needs them.
