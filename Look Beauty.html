Privacy Policy

Look Beauty · Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short: Look Beauty does not collect, send or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Look Beauty app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. The app is provided by Zackelevy LLC.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not have accounts, servers or a database, and we cannot see what you enter in the app.

2. Information you enter in the app

The app lets you save the following information, which you choose to enter:

  • Your routine steps and which steps you have ticked
  • Your streak and daily water count
  • Product names, opening dates and how many months they last
  • Wishlist items
  • Diary entries (a mood rating from 1 to 5 and an optional note)
  • Your theme choice (Light, Dark or System)

This information is stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, sent to us or shared with anyone.

3. Permissions, internet and third parties

  • The app does not request any permissions. It does not access your camera, photos, files, contacts, location or notifications.
  • The app does not connect to the internet.
  • The app does not contain ads, analytics, tracking tools or third-party services.

4. How we use information

Because we do not receive your information, we do not use it for any purpose. The app uses what you enter only to show it back to you on your device.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not have access to it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time in any of these ways:

  • Remove single items, such as steps, products, wishlist items or diary entries, inside the app.
  • Open Settings in the app and choose Reset all data to remove everything you have added.
  • Clear the app’s storage in your phone settings, or uninstall the app.

Because the data is only on your device, we cannot recover it for you once it is deleted. The app does not back up your data to us. Your device’s own backup service (for example, Android backup) may copy app data depending on your phone settings; that is controlled by you and your device provider, not by us.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Not medical advice

Look Beauty is a personal organiser. It does not provide medical, dermatological or professional advice. Expiry dates shown in the app are calculated from the dates and months you enter. Always follow the instructions and expiry information on the product packaging, and speak to a qualified professional about any skin concerns.

9. Security

Your information is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot normally read. No method of storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and up-to-date software.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this page. If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
