Privacy Policy

FlowFarm: Irrigation Log · Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FlowFarm: Irrigation Log Android app ("FlowFarm", "the app") handles information. The app is provided by Zackelevy LLC ("we", "us", "our"). In short: FlowFarm works offline, stores your records only on your device, and does not collect or share your personal information.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, receive, transmit or sell any personal information. FlowFarm has no user accounts, does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location, and does not connect to any server operated by us.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the records you enter on your own device only. This includes:

  • Field details (name, crop, size, flow rate and weekly water target)
  • Watering entries (field, date, duration, volume, soil condition and notes)
  • Rainfall entries (date, amount and notes)
  • Equipment care tasks and their dates
  • App settings (unit system, water price and currency symbol)

This information stays in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to any third party by the app.

3. Permissions

FlowFarm does not request any special Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location, and it does not need an internet connection.

4. Advertising, analytics and third-party services

FlowFarm contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that collect data. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

5. Sharing of information

Because we do not collect your information, we do not sell, rent, trade or share it with anyone.

6. Backups

Depending on your device settings, Android may include app data in your device backups (for example, a backup to your Google account). Such backups are managed by your device and its operating system provider under their own privacy terms, not by us. You can manage or turn off backups in your device settings.

7. Your control and data deletion

You are in control of your data. You can delete individual entries, fields and tasks inside the app at any time. You can also delete everything at once with the "Reset all data" button in the app menu. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data from your device.

8. Data security

Your records are kept in the app's private storage, which is protected by the Android operating system. Because the data never leaves your device through the app, its security also depends on how well you protect your device (for example, with a screen lock).

9. Children's privacy

FlowFarm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Estimates and disclaimer

FlowFarm is a record-keeping tool for informational purposes. Water volume, depth and cost figures are estimates based on the information you enter and may not reflect actual consumption. The app does not replace professional agricultural advice.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.
