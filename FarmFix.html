FarmFix Privacy Policy

App: FarmFix – Farm Equipment Maintenance · Last updated: October 30, 2026
In short: FarmFix stores equipment, maintenance, usage, and expense records locally on your device. It does not require an account, internet connection, or external service. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your data.

1. Who we are

FarmFix is a farm equipment maintenance tracker published by Zackelevy LLC (“we”, “us”). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at abati1999am@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores

FarmFix stores only the information you type into it, and only on your device:

  • Equipment details (name, category, brand, model, serial number, purchase date and price, notes).
  • Operating-hour or usage readings and their dates.
  • Maintenance schedules, service history, replaced parts, technician names, repair notes, and costs.
  • Your app preferences (currency symbol, date format, default unit, due-soon thresholds, delete-confirmation setting).

This information is saved in a private local database that only FarmFix can access. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect personal information, and the app does not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • The app does not request any Android permissions and does not access the internet.

4. Sharing of information

Because your data never leaves your device through the app, we do not share it with third parties, advertisers, or service providers. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

5. Data storage and security

Your records are kept in the app’s private storage on your device and are protected by Android’s app sandbox. We recommend using your device’s screen lock to protect your data. Because we do not receive your data, we cannot access, recover, or restore it for you.

If you have enabled your device’s own backup features (for example, Android or Google backup), your device provider may include app data in those backups under the provider’s own terms. This is controlled by your device settings, not by FarmFix.

6. Your choices and control

  • Edit or delete records: you can edit or delete any equipment, schedule, usage reading, or history entry inside the app.
  • Delete everything: go to Settings → “Delete all app data”. This permanently erases all records and preferences and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall: removing the app from your device also removes its local data.

7. Children’s privacy

FarmFix is a general-purpose record-keeping tool and is not directed at children. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Disclaimer

FarmFix is a record-keeping tool for informational purposes. Maintenance schedules and cost summaries depend on the information entered by the user and do not replace manufacturer instructions or professional equipment servicing advice.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above. If a future version of the app adds features that involve collecting or transmitting data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
