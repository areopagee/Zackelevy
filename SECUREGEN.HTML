Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026  ·  App version 1.0.0

In short: SecureGen generates passwords locally on your device. It does not require an account, connect to the internet, collect analytics, or send generated passwords to external services.

1. Who we are

SecureGen (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an offline password, passphrase and PIN generator for Android, published by Zackelevy LLC. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

2. Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, receive, transmit or share any personal information. We do not operate servers for the app, and the app has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking components.

3. Generated passwords, passphrases and PINs

  • Every value is created on your device using Java’s SecureRandom cryptographic random number generator.
  • Generated values are kept in the device’s memory only while you are using the app.
  • They are never written to storage, logged, uploaded, or added to a history.
  • Closing the app, or generating a new value, discards the previous one.

4. Information stored on your device

The app saves only two non-sensitive preferences in its private storage, which other apps cannot read:

SettingPurpose
Default password lengthUsed when the app starts and when you reset settings.
Exclude ambiguous charactersRemembers your on/off choice.

These settings contain no passwords and no personal data. Use Settings → Reset settings in the app, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove them.

5. Clipboard

The app uses the Android clipboard only when you tap Copy (or tap the password area). It never reads the clipboard. On supported Android versions the copied value is marked as sensitive so the system can hide it in previews.

Once copied, the value lives on the system clipboard under Android’s control, outside this app. Other apps and clipboard managers on your device may be able to access it, so paste it promptly and clear your clipboard if you are concerned.

6. Permissions and network access

SecureGen requests no Android permissions. It does not declare the internet permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the network. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

7. Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or tracking, and no data is shared with any third party.

8. Strength estimates

The strength meter is an estimate based on length and character variety. It is not a guarantee that a password is secure. Choose a unique password for every account and consider using a reputable password manager to store them.

9. Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be reflected in a new app version.
